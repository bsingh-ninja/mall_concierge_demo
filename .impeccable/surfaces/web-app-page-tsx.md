# Mall Concierge: shopper app and operator console

Mode: operate. Audience: client executives watching a presenter; shoppers on a phone via QR. Job: plan a mall visit from a sentence, walk it, survive a lunch-crowd reroute; operator sees footfall, conversions, tenants below target, agent trace.
Chosen after three re-rolls: the category standard, executed at the craft of Google Maps indoor, the Westfield app and Mappedin. User steer: "user friendly, show them a map and everything".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The map is the app. Every store is named and iconed on a calm indoor map; the plan, the walk and the reroute live in a pull-up sheet over it. Refuses decorative worlds, dashboards-first and text-only itineraries.

OWN-WORLD: Strict minimalism (user distill brief, binding): neutral greys and white, one accent #1a73e8 used only for the primary action and the route, semantic red #d93025 only for the crowd surge and always with text. No cards, no drop shadows, no borders or dividers; hierarchy from whitespace and type alone. Inter, four sizes (13/15/20/32), weights 400/500/600, tabular figures. The map stays the core: light grey floor, white units, grey icons, store names wherever they fit.

STORY: The shopper sees the whole mall at once, types or taps a request, watches the agents plan, gets a blue route and four photo stops with prices, walks it with a turn banner, and gets a reroute card with old vs new time and a coupon. The executive flips to the operator console and sees the same map as a footfall heatmap with the tenants that benefited.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: full-bleed labelled map; one flat white prompt field on top with the concierge reply as plain text and text-link suggestions; flat map controls on the right; a flat white sheet at the bottom with offers as plain rows and the single blue Plan action. Desktop stage: the phone centred on a quiet grey page, story steps and agent log as plain text columns, mode switch as text.

FORM: canon (category standard), standing exit taken by the user after re-rolls; seed:06ac81cb69101489.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
